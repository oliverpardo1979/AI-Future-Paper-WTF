## Attribution checks

- **Jones and Tonetti (2026), version 0.5, May 2026.** Existing cached full
  text and current author PDF checked. Section 5 endogenizes automation in
  goods and ideas; one calibration continues historical automation. Weak
  links delay aggregate acceleration. This is not a claim that their
  task-level elasticity equals this paper's aggregate elasticity, or that
  their transition solves this paper's decentralized equilibrium problem.
  Source: https://web.stanford.edu/~chadj/JonesTonetti_Automation.pdf
- **Erdil et al. (2025), GATE.** Existing cached full text, especially
  Sections 3.6 and 5.1, checked against the current arXiv record. Compute
  expansion and conventional capital investment face adjustment costs.
  The allocation is chosen by a planner. No claim is made that adjustment
  costs eliminate every initial jump or are present in our current model.
  Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.04941

## Editorial changes

The related-literature section now explains GATE's adjustment costs, the
ongoing-automation interpretation in Jones and Tonetti, and the distinction
between complementary investment, measured productivity, and technological
news. Section 6.1 cites these mechanisms while making clear that the current
experiments do not include them. Two published bibliography entries were
added. No new simulation, calibration target, time-varying chi, adjustment
cost, or initial-condition restriction was introduced.
