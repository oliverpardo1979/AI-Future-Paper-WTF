# Completion of the literature tool, 16 September 2026

## Scope

Complete the bibliographic inventory and expose the connections already discussed
with the author. This is not a claim to have read or independently verified every
page of every listed work. Manuscript files and simulation artifacts are untouched.

## Content

- 59 targeted connection records, including the Jones/top-five candidates.
- All 31 entries of Davidson's bibliography are now represented (13 previously
  deferred additions), without duplicating earlier and later versions already
  present under a different year/key.
- Davidson: feedback loops, inference compute in research, hyperbolic growth,
  finite-time singularity, and limits of transferring fixed-allocation results.
- Jones--Tonetti: innovation/automation feedback and weak links, distinguishing
  endogenous technology from fixed research expenditure shares.
- Korinek--McKelvey and Sastry: inference/training terminology versus the
  economic destination of compute; no assertion that research compute is a
  universal technical category excluding inference.
- GATE and the innovation literature: investment and research allocation have
  antecedents; private investment alone is not a defensible novelty claim.
- Wage/labor-share and delayed-takeoff precedents, with proposed locations and
  comparison limits, are visible rather than buried in audit notes.

Reused the original-passage audits of 13 and 16 September and the Jones/top-five
review with their version/access caveats. Rechecked Autor--Kausik's Theorem 1
and Benjamin Jones's discussion of a given research budget. The immediately
preceding terminology and section 5.3 checks provide the Davidson,
Korinek--McKelvey and Sastry locators recorded in the database.

The 13 new inventory items were identified from Davidson's actual reference list
(printed pp. 44--46) and primary author/publisher pages where available. Their
full-text reading remains explicitly pending. Roodman's 2020 citation is not
silently replaced by the 2026 arXiv deposit. The AI-generated note's provenance
is disclosed; no theorem is adopted from it.

## Integrity

The builder merges the frozen attribution audit with newer explicit connection
reviews. DOI metadata alone never marks a source as read. The browser exposes
reading status separately from bibliographic verification and dates of build.
Coverage, duplicate keys/DOIs, unknown connection keys, and partially populated
structured reviews are validated. The local and public-site copies are built
from the same data. No PDF or manuscript rebuild is necessary for this update.
