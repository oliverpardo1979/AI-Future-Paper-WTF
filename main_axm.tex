\title{The Future of Growth and Human Labor under Automated AI Research:\\
AI Capability as Research-Augmenting Technology}
\author{Oliver Pardo}
\date{August 2026}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
I extend the Ramsey--Cass--Koopmans model to study how the substitutability
between AI and labor affects economic growth, wages, interest rates, and factor
shares. A monopolistic AI developer supplies AI services and invests in better
AI using human and AI research. When AI and labor are complements, labor remains
a bottleneck, and AI generates no growth in output per person beyond exogenous
labor productivity growth. At unit elasticity, sustained endogenous growth is
possible only when the feedback from AI capability to AI research is
sufficiently weak. When AI and labor are substitutes, I characterize a
conditional finite-boundary path along which output and the interest rate become
unbounded in finite time, while real wages rise and labor's income share
converges to zero. Near unit elasticity, these differences can take a very long
time to emerge, so moderate growth may persist before a sharp acceleration.
\end{abstract}
