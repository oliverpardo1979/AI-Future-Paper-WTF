\title{The Future of Growth and Human Labor\\
Under Autonomous AI Research}
\author{Oliver Pardo}
\date{August 2026}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
I extend the Ramsey--Cass--Koopmans model with a monopolistic developer that
supplies AI services and invests in better AI. I use the model to study how
substitution between AI and labor affects growth, wages, interest rates, and
factor shares. When AI and labor are complements, labor remains a bottleneck,
and output per person grows only with exogenous labor productivity. At unit
elasticity, sustained endogenous growth requires sufficiently weak feedback
from AI capability to AI research. When AI and labor are substitutes, I
characterize a conditional branch of the necessary conditions along which
output and the interest rate become unbounded in finite time and labor's income
share converges to zero. This branch is not an established infinite-horizon
equilibrium and is not reported as a simulated equilibrium trajectory. A
one-sided equilibrium comparison below unit elasticity shows that departures
from the unit-elastic benchmark can take a very long time to emerge.
\end{abstract}
