\title{The Future of Growth and Human Labor\\
Under Recursive AI Self-Improvement}
\author{Oliver Pardo}

\begin{abstract}
I develop an endogenous-growth model in which a monopolistic developer supplies
effective AI production services and uses effective AI research services to
improve AI efficiency, creating recursive self-improvement. I characterize
infinite-horizon equilibria with a finite AI-efficiency frontier. Long-run
outcomes depend jointly on the substitutability between AI and labor and on the
level of that frontier. When labor remains a production bottleneck, output per
person and real wages grow at the exogenous rate of labor-augmenting
technological progress, and labor retains a positive income share. With
sufficient substitution and a sufficiently high frontier, the economy instead
becomes AI-dominated: real-wage growth exceeds this exogenous rate, but output
per person grows even faster. Labor's income share therefore converges to zero,
and long-run growth and interest rates rise with the frontier. Numerical
equilibrium paths show that moderate growth can persist for a long time before
output accelerates and labor's income share falls sharply.
\end{abstract}
