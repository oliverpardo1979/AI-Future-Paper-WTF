The fitted value is $\chi=7.616305$. Initial output-per-person growth is
$1.62\%$, $2.71\%$, $3.02\%$, and $4.54\%$ across the four elasticities,
compared with $1\%$ before activation.
Output, wages and prices remain continuous at activation, but growth
rates adjust immediately. The central scenario therefore retains an
abrupt response to the unexpected availability of RSI.

At $\sigma=1.50$, consumption rises by $5.05\%$ at activation and
research initially absorbs $3.10\%$ of output. Developer net profit is
positive at $0.20\%$ of output, and gross investment is $13.79\%$ of output.
This case requires neither initial developer losses nor negative gross
investment. Investment is nevertheless below depreciation, so the capital stock
initially falls at an annualized rate of $0.82\%$. These are equilibrium
outcomes, not added financing or investment constraints.

At $\sigma=1.50$, labor's income share reaches half its initial value after
approximately 47 years. It completes $90\%$ of its decline toward zero after
about 137 years. Output-per-person growth, real-wage growth and the interest
rate converge to $3.13\%$, $2.42\%$, and $7.13\%$, respectively. In the other
three scenarios, output-per-person and real-wage growth return to $1\%$,
and the interest rate returns to $5\%$.
